为了向您提供文档拍摄扫描、导入及导出 PDF 等基础功能，本应用会在您主动触发相关功能时申请以下系统权限：

   - **使用场景与目的：** 仅在您点击“扫描/拍照”按钮时使用，用于拍摄纸质文档、收据、名片或笔记。

2. **存储与文件读写权限 (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**
   - **使用场景与目的：** 用于导入您主动选择的本地 PDF/图片文件，以及将合并、拆分、压缩或生成的 PDF 文件保存到您的手机存储（如 Documents 或 Downloads 目录）。
   - **处理方式：** 本应用仅访问您明确选择的文件，不会扫描或读取您设备中的其他无关私人文件。
3. **网络访问权限 (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**
   - **使用场景与目的：** 仅用于华为应用市场应用内购买（HMS Core IAP）验证订单状态及检查应用版本更新。

### 2. 100% On-Device Processing & Zero Personal Data Upload
PDFCreator is engineered with a **privacy-first, on-device architecture**:
- **We do not upload, store, sell, or transmit your documents, scans, or personal files to any external cloud servers.**
- You do not need to register an account or provide your name, phone number, or location to use the App's core document tools.

### 3. Device Permissions and Purpose of Use
To deliver core document scanning and PDF creation functionalities, the App requests access to specific device features only when triggered by your actions:

1. **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**
   - **Purpose:** Used solely to take photos of physical documents, receipts, ID cards, or notes when you initiate a scan.
   - **Data Handling:** All camera frames and captured images are processed strictly in local device memory and never leave your device.
2. **Storage / Files & Documents (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**
   - **Purpose:** Used strictly to open existing PDF/image files you select and to save exported PDF files to your device storage (`Documents` or `Downloads`).
   - **Data Handling:** The App only accesses files you explicitly pick and never scans unrelated personal files.
3. **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**
   - **Purpose:** Used strictly to verify in-app purchases/subscriptions via Huawei HMS Core IAP or Google Play Billing and to check for app updates.

### 4. Local Processing & On-Device OCR
- **On-Device OCR:** Optical character recognition (OCR) and text extraction are performed entirely on your device using local libraries. No documents, images, or extracted text are transmitted to external servers.

### 5. Third-Party SDKs & In-App Purchases (Huawei HMS Core IAP)
- **No Ad Networks or Tracking SDKs:** PDFCreator contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero behavioral profiling trackers.
- **Huawei HMS Core In-App Purchases (`com.huawei.hms:iap`):**
  - On Huawei devices, upgrading to PDFCreator Pro or restoring purchases utilizes the official **Huawei HMS Core IAP SDK**.
  - **Data Processed:** Purchase history tokens, subscription status, and order verification identifiers managed by Huawei AppGallery to unlock VIP features and restore purchases. Financial billing credentials are handled exclusively by Huawei Pay and are never accessible to the Developer.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all PDFs, images, and documents reside locally on your device:
- You retain complete ownership and control over your data.
- Deleting a document within the App or uninstalling the App permanently removes the App's locally stored files from your device.

### 7. Children's Privacy
PDFCreator does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 14 in the Chinese mainland).
